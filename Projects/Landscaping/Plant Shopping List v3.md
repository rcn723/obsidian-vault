## Drip Emitter Notes

- **Emitter type:** Use PRESSURE-COMPENSATING emitters throughout. The sloped yard means standard emitters won't maintain consistent flow across the bed.
- **Camellias (2 GPH × 2 each):** Place one emitter on each side of the root ball, 6–8 inches out. Ensures even saturation across the larger root zone.
- **Epimedium — dry zone:** Add 0.5–1 GPH emitters, one per every 2 plants, tied to the same zone as Sarcococa and Camellia. Same timer, one less zone to manage.
- **Ajuga — middle zone:** Shares Skimmia-row emitters. No dedicated emitters needed.
- **Drip season:** Activate mid-May, deactivate mid-October.

---

## Watering — Year 1 vs. Established

> **Year 1 warning:** Do NOT rely on residual drip alone. Ground covers are shallow-rooted during establishment and will fail in the dry zone without dedicated watering Jun–Sep.

**Year 1 (establishment, Jun–Sep):**
- Epimedium, back row dry zone: 2×/week. Most vulnerable — don't skip.
- Epimedium, corner wrap: 1×/week. Deeper shade keeps it moister.
- Ajuga, middle zone: 1×/week via shared shrub emitters.

**Year 2+ (established):**
- Epimedium: Beaverton rainfall (Oct–May) handles most of the year. In drought summers (Jul–Aug) one deep watering per week is sufficient. Largely self-sufficient.
- Ajuga: Fine on shared shrub drip + natural rainfall. No dedicated emitters needed long-term.

## Nursery Tips

- **Skimmia japonica:** Verify male vs. female labelling before purchasing — ask staff directly, labels aren't always reliable.
- **Camellias:** 5-gallon specimens ($80–120 each) give 2–3 years of growth already done. Commit to annual pruning each February or they'll press against the foundation within 3–4 years.
- **Epimedium:** 4-inch pots are fine and significantly cheaper ($3–5 vs $8–12 each). Any variety works in PNW dry shade — x rubrum and 'Frohnleiten' are widely available at Portland-area nurseries.
- **Ajuga 'Black Scallop':** $3–5 each in 4-inch pots. Confirm 'Black Scallop' specifically (near-black foliage) — not generic Ajuga reptans.
- **Existing starts:** Flag the 4–6 hellebore starts from 7 Dees before planting. Extend the drift with new plants, don't replace what's there. Keep Ajuga at least 12" from any Hellebore crown.
