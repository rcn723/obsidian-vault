Watering: See watering card below.

Watering Guide

Year 1 — Establishment
!

Residual drip alone is not enough in year 1. Ground covers need consistent moisture Apr–Oct while they root in.

1

Dry zone (Epimedium near foundation): Add 2–3 low-flow drip emitters (0.5–1 GPH) per 4 ft of back row. Run 2×/week June–Sept. This is the area most likely to fail without attention.

2

Middle zone (Ajuga): Can share emitters already serving the Skimmia row. 1×/week deep watering June–Sept is enough in year 1.

3

Corner wrap (Epimedium): Shade is deeper here — 1×/week June–Sept. Less drought pressure than main bed.

Year 2+ — Established
✓

Epimedium: Beaverton rainfall (Oct–May) carries it through most of the year. July–Aug may need one supplemental deep watering per week in drought years. Largely self-sufficient.

✓

Ajuga: Fine on shared shrub drip + natural rainfall after year 2. No dedicated emitters needed long-term.

Practical tip: Connect the back-row Epimedium emitters to the same drip zone as the Sarcococa and Camellias. Same frequency, same timer. One less zone to manage.

Planting Order

1

Flag existing starts so you don't plant over them.

2

Place Camellias first — step back from the patio chairs and confirm position before digging.

3

Set Sarcococa in the back row gaps, especially driest zone near foundation.

4

Plant Skimmia (middle row). Male central between the two females.

5

Add Daphne at corner — position where you'll walk past it toward the patio.

6

Group Helleborus into a drift, working around existing starts.

7

Plant Carex at patio edge, Hostas in between.

8

Plant Epimedium in back row gaps (12–15" spacing) and throughout corner wrap.

9

Plant Ajuga in middle zone (10–12" spacing). Install edge strip at patio-side boundary.

10

Top-dress all bare soil with 2–3" arborist chip mulch. Keep clear of plant crowns. Mulch covers gaps while ground covers fill in (takes 1–2 seasons).
