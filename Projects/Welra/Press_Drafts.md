## 5. Google Form — "The 5-Minute Seller Analytics Survey"

**Form title:** The 5-Minute Seller Analytics Survey
**Form description:**
> A quick anonymous study on how online sellers actually use (or avoid) their shop stats. 6 questions, ~3 minutes, no email required. I'll share the full results free with every community this was posted in. — Ryan, a fellow seller

**Settings:** don't collect email addresses; allow anyone with the link.

**Questions (types in parentheses):**
1. Which platforms do you sell on? *(Checkboxes)* — Etsy / Shopify / WooCommerce / Amazon / TikTok Shop / Square / In-person (markets, fairs) / Other
2. Roughly how many hours per WEEK do you spend looking at stats or analytics across all of them? *(Multiple choice)* — 0, I don't look / Under 1 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / More than 10
3. How often do you check your numbers and come away unsure what to actually DO? *(Multiple choice)* — Never / Sometimes / About half the time / Usually / Always
4. Have you ever stopped checking your stats entirely for a month or more? *(Multiple choice)* — Yes / No
5. What's your monthly revenue range, all channels combined? *(Multiple choice)* — Under $1k / $1–5k / $5–20k / $20–50k / Over $50k / Prefer not to say
6. In one sentence: what's the most confusing thing about your shop's numbers? *(Paragraph)*
7. *(Optional, last)* Want the results when they're ready? Drop an email — used for nothing else. *(Short answer, NOT required)*

**Distribution post (Reddit/FB — adapt the first line per community):**
> **Doing a tiny study: how many hours do you actually spend on your shop stats every week?**
> Fellow seller here (Etsy + POD). I'm trying to find out how much time we all collectively burn staring at dashboards — and whether anyone actually knows what to do with what they see. 6 questions, anonymous, ~3 minutes: [form link]
> I'll post the full results back here free once there are enough responses. No catch — I'm genuinely curious whether it's just me.

(Note: this post contains no Welra link — that's deliberate. The results post later is where credibility gets built.)
